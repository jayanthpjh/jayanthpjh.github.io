FORWARD-DEPLOYED METHOD

From discovery to deployment.

Reliable systems are built in the gap
between source and destination.

01

Understand the source

Trace file formats, event shapes, business keys, and the edge cases upstream systems hide.

02

Map and transform

Design the bronze, silver, and gold layers with explicit grain, ownership, and failure behavior.

03

Validate and reconcile

Make missing records, duplicates, invalid mappings, and rejected rows impossible to ignore.

04

Cut over safely

Use repeatable backfills, observable runs, and post-conversion checks so production is a controlled step.
